## Finding an Idea

As I've continued learning Java and exploring its many applications, I knew I wanted a real project to apply what I was learning to. I've also been interested in B2C business for a while now, so I decided to combine the two and start one.

## How Java and Other Languages Will Be Used

At the core of this business is a database of inventory connected to a website that lets customers check whether a given product is in stock. Since each main product can be customized with different parts and modifications, the inventory grows more complex than a typical single-SKU store — which makes keeping it accurate and up to date absolutely critical.

## Legal

To get this project off the ground, I'll be forming an LLC. Founding a company brings a long list of new tasks: drafting Articles of Organization, structuring the company, filing with the state, handling taxes, securing product insurance, and more. All of this has to be in place before the business can actually operate.

## General Structure

The plan is to source the main product along with the parts and add-ons customers can include or remove, then sell everything through a website with live product images that update as customers modify their configuration. Each time a customer adjusts their build, the inventory database will check stock in real time. Once an order is placed, I'll assemble the product to spec and ship it out.

## Current Roadblocks

Drafting the Articles of Organization is one of the biggest hurdles right now — it needs to be done carefully to avoid problems down the road. Alongside that, checking for copyright and trademark conflicts in naming the business is a top priority before moving forward.

## Summary

This is a substantial undertaking, but it's also a chance to learn firsthand how a business comes together, from the earliest legal groundwork to the moment it starts selling products. Along the way, I'll be using modern industry languages and plugins to build the website, which will give me hands-on experience with tools I'm still working to master.
