## Starting a Business

Everyone has had the idea to start a business online, but few people actually commit to turning that idea into a legitimate business. Starting a business requires more than just having an idea. It requires planning, research, time, money, and the willingness to deal with problems as they come up. With my knowledge of programming and software development, I decided to take the idea of starting a business seriously and begin working toward creating one of my own.

## First Steps

Before starting a legitimate business, there are several things that need to be considered and completed first. I need to decide what type of business structure to use, what products I want to sell, what supplies I need to purchase, how I will handle orders, and how customers will find the business. I also need to consider costs, pricing, taxes, shipping, advertising, and other responsibilities that come with operating a business.

I decided to use a B2C (business-to-consumer) model. The main idea is to purchase watches, primarily the G-SHOCK GA-2100, and modify them based on a customer's order. Customers will be able to choose different modifications and create a watch that fits their preferences. In addition to custom orders, I plan to offer pre-made watches that are already modified. These pre-made watches can be sold at a discount compared to fully custom orders, giving customers a more affordable option while also allowing me to sell inventory that has already been completed.

## Why this?

I have always loved watches and have been interested in the reasoning behind different models, including their purpose, design, functionality, and appearance. Watches are more than just something used to tell time; they can also be a way for someone to express their personal style. This is one of the reasons I became interested in modifying watches.

I have known about G-SHOCK modification for some time, but I had never actually tried it myself. The GA-2100 especially interests me because of its design and the number of ways it can be customized. Starting a business around something I already have an interest in gives me an opportunity to learn more about watches while also developing business and technical skills. It also allows me to combine my interest in watches with my experience in programming and software development.

## Next Steps

After deciding to use an LLC, one of the first major steps is creating an operating agreement. An operating agreement establishes how the LLC will be managed and how important decisions will be handled. It can cover topics such as ownership, responsibilities, financial contributions, distribution of profits, and procedures for making business decisions.

The operating agreement and other business documents need to be created carefully and in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations. Taking the time to properly establish the business now can help prevent problems in the future. I also need to make sure that the business's finances are properly separated from my personal finances and that I understand the tax and financial responsibilities associated with operating an LLC.

## What's Next?

Next, I need to file the Articles of Organization with the appropriate government office to officially establish the LLC. Once the LLC is established, I can begin working on the operational side of the business. This includes ordering inventory, purchasing the tools and materials needed to modify the watches, determining pricing, and developing a system for tracking inventory and orders.

Another major part of the process will be creating the website. Since I have experience with programming and software development, I can use those skills to create a website specifically designed for the business. The website will need to allow customers to view available watches, learn about customization options, place orders, and contact the business.

I will also create social media accounts for the business to advertise products and reach potential customers. These accounts can be used to show completed modifications, introduce new designs, announce available pre-made watches, and build a recognizable brand. Eventually, the goal is to have the legal business, website, inventory, ordering system, and social media presence all working together as one operation.
